## Matcher findings

- MLRS alerts use `РСЗВ` both before and after the area name.
- Guided-bomb alerts use `КАБ`, `КАБи`, `КАБів`, `КАБу`, `КАБа`, `КАБами`,
  hyphenated forms, and official `керованих авіаційних бомб` wording.
- The exact `🟡💣` pair can replace the weapon name in terse locality-only
  guided-bomb alerts.
- Forecast, analysis, aftermath, and all-clear wording was collected separately
  and remains inactive in detector tests.
- `nebo_raketa` returned no `РСЗВ` result during this pass; all seven listed
  channels were still searched.
